# Tamil Nadu data and validation operations

Status checked 8 November 2026. The goal is a statewide product; build and independently check a small repeatable district/land-class pilot first, then expand within Tamil Nadu, then to Maharashtra. A government reference rate, a sale, a listing, a project registration and an image are different observations. None alone establishes the price of a particular parcel.

## Existing evidence and current access

| Source | What it can provide | Status and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |

Historical collection previously passed through public proxies without certificate verification: independently sample-check records and geographic matches before reliance. Guideline revisions are not market transactions. |
| [Tamil Nadu RERA](https://rera.tn.gov.in/) | Registered project details and dates, subject to actual access and terms | The repository has 2,127 unique IDs from incomplete building and layout extracts: 267 dated 2024, 1,508 in 2025 and 352 in 2026; all 1,117 layout rows are from January–April 2025. Coordinates are not independently verified. The current 2024 and legacy 2018 building pages returned HTTP 503 from this environment. This is not complete RERA coverage, and a registered project's proposed cost is not verified spending or a land sale. |
| TN registered documents | Potential dated, recorded consideration where the actual document provides it | Scope, costs, reuse rights, geographic coverage and historical availability must be confirmed with the registration department or a permitted provider. Tamil Nadu describes free online EC viewing/downloading in its [official government report](https://www.tamilarasu.org/emagazines/assets/pdf/free_copies/2_Year_Achievement_English_2023_Book.pdf), while the [application guide](https://www.tnreginet.gov.in/portal/document/ec_portal.pdf) describes a separate paid application processed by an SRO. Neither establishes a free bulk transaction feed. Encumbrance records alone may not contain all fields needed for a comparable sale. Protect party and parcel identifiers. |

Collection dates may be confused with event dates; check source rights before collecting or redistributing listings. | [Landsat](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions) and [Sentinel-2](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/sentinel-2-l2a) | Historical and recent images and possible land-cover context | Historical imagery can help characterize changes in built-up land, but is not a land-price series or proof of title. Record sensor, resolution, observation date, cloud and quality flags. |

On 8 November 2026, two reproducible read-only spot checks (seeds `2026-W45` and `2026-W45-B`) compared 28 distinct saved searches across revisions from 2002 to 2024 against direct certificate-verified official responses. All 28 matched across 118 saved street rows. These samples were restricted to searches with 1–10 rows and do not validate large searches, all districts, village-to-map matches, or the five unresolved records.

The Dindigul district administration published a [draft guideline-value notice](https://dindigul.nic.in/draft-guideline-value-prepared-for-revenue-villages/) on 24 September 2026. It describes proposed corrections available for public inspection, not enacted rates or a statewide revision. Check the official register and effective dates before changing values in the app; do not treat a proposal as a completed transaction or market price.

RERA is worth backfilling as a project-activity source; it does not replace a transaction panel. The available RERA extract should first be checked against official pages for registration ID, dates, type, location and terms. Historical pages must be discovered and parsed by year and category from actual successful responses before any unattended collector is enabled. A 503 or an empty/error page is not an empty year.

## Current scheduled work

`.github/workflows/tn-source-retry.yml` runs once weekly or manually. It checks two RERA page responses and retries at most five parked TN guideline searches with at most 20 guideline requests for those retries. A separate spot-check makes at most 14 guideline requests, and the RERA check requests two pages. Each guideline stage stops after three consecutive connection failures, and retry starting points rotate weekly so later entries are not permanently skipped. It uses verified HTTPS, checks pagination and source counts, and saves only complete, valid positive result sets with an official URL and retrieval time. If nothing is recovered it writes no dataset and commits nothing. Raw-only recoveries do not redeploy the app; a reviewed location match and history build are needed before any app update. It also compares up to two small, deterministically selected saved searches per revision against the live official portal; the sample excludes larger searches and is not a statewide accuracy estimate. It does not scrape RERA or market data. Recoveries are left unplaced until their location is independently matched; they do not silently appear as point-level app history. A final health gate fails the run if the sample is incomplete, different or unavailable, or either RERA page could not be checked; it runs after any verified recoveries are saved. Review the workflow's run summary for source failures.

The first GitHub-hosted run on 8 November timed out on both RERA pages and every attempted guideline request, and wrote no data. A one-shot GitHub runner in this workspace subsequently reached the guideline portal: all 14 small sampled searches matched, while the five parked searches returned error pages and RERA returned 503. The temporary runner deregistered after its job; it is not a persistent scheduled service. On 9 November the scheduled GitHub-hosted check again reached neither RERA nor the first three guideline samples. That old run was marked successful despite the outage; the new health gate corrects future reporting. Access depends on the execution network. An appropriately authorized runner or service with verified TLS and reliable portal connectivity is needed for unattended recovery; the scheduled GitHub job alone does not guarantee collection. Do not bypass authentication, access controls or certificate checks.

The scheduled Tamil Nadu retry and economic refresh use GitHub-hosted runners unless the repository Actions variable `BHOOMI_PORTAL_SCHEDULED` is explicitly set to `true`. After an appropriately isolated, maintained runner with label `bhoomi-portal` and the required tools has been connected and tested, that variable routes these two scheduled jobs to it. The runner must have network access to the sources, verified TLS, Python 3.12, Node.js, npm, git and GitHub CLI. Do not put personal records or provider credentials on a public-repository runner, and do not run untrusted pull-request code on it. If the runner is offline, jobs routed to it will queue; remove or unset the variable to return schedules to GitHub-hosted runners. No persistent runner is currently configured. The separately scheduled district CPI workflow stays on GitHub-hosted infrastructure by default.

The old large, parallel guideline collector remains disabled; do not restart it merely to repeat already collected revisions. The separate news workflow finds leads and is not a verified project-status or transaction feed. The Gazette refresh is paused pending clarification of the third-party source's redistribution terms or a verified official-source replacement; see [SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md).

## Additional economic sources

The monthly `.github/workflows/macro-data.yml` refresh uses the official MoSPI eSankhyiki API for combined rural/urban general CPI in Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra and India and the World Bank Indicators API for five national economic and population series. These are consumer prices and national context, not land prices or district observations. On 8 November the checked CPI snapshot contained 758 observations, with 2012-base and 2024-base series kept separate and official back-series labelled. The 2024-base back-series supplied by the API covers India only. Missing months are not filled; the 2024-base API does not provide a final/provisional flag in the records returned here, and the latest month can be provisional. The World Bank series retain missing values and cite its [data terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use). Both sources are independently checked; a failed source keeps its previous snapshot, and unchanged values do not create a commit. The workflow saves any verified updates before reporting a partial source outage as a failed health check. In the first GitHub-hosted run the World Bank API succeeded, while the MoSPI API was unreachable. A subsequent manual run through a one-shot `bhoomi-portal` runner successfully checked both sources and found no changes; the runner removed itself afterward. A persistent runner with suitable connectivity would need separately managed hosting, Python 3.12, Node.js, npm, git and GitHub CLI. Observation dates are not release dates, and revised data cannot be used as if they were the information known at a historical forecast date without point-in-time vintages.

The Tamil Nadu Department of Economics and Statistics publishes a separate [district consumer-price series](https://des.tn.gov.in/en/node/24), with a 2019–2020 base. The checked report available on 9 November contained 1,353 combined-sector monthly observations for 32 named areas and the state from January 2023 through May 2026. It is older than the state-level MoSPI snapshot and measures household consumer prices, not land prices. The publication's 32 area names have not been matched to today's district boundaries; the app presents them as a separate source rather than assigning values to map districts. The state series is not directly comparable to the national CPI's differently based series. The monthly `.github/workflows/tn-district-cpi.yml` discovers the current report on the official publication page, validates the base, area rows and month coverage, and retains the last snapshot on errors. It warns when the latest observation is four or more months old, commits only changed observations or source metadata, and needs `pdftotext` (Poppler) on a connected runner. These revised observations do not establish what was known at a historical forecast date.

The RBI publishes a [city house-price index](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_PressRelease.aspx?prid=63436) from registration data, and NHB publishes house-price indices. These are useful context, not a vacant-land transaction series. Reuse and redistribution rights for source series need to be confirmed before mirroring them in this public app.

A possible transaction-data supplier is [LandLens by Verified.RealEstate](https://verified.realestate/landlens/docs/land-price-api). Its documentation advertises Tamil Nadu registered-sale results back to 2001, a four-credit parcel report and a separate enterprise/bulk plan. This is a provider claim, not independently checked coverage or verified market consideration. A private, zero-credit connection probe on 13 November successfully authenticated and called the parcel and guideline endpoints. A guideline-history lookup by point returned `not_found`; the provider's documented street example returned five revisions from 2007 to 2024. Those calls do not establish statewide coverage, current rates or source accuracy. Its free transaction endpoint only reads completed Price Maps already on the account, and returns up to 250 deeds per kind, newest and priced first.

LandLens's published [terms, sections 6.3–6.4](https://verified.realestate/landlens/terms), limit reports to personal or internal business use and restrict reproduction, derivatives, public display and commercial exploitation without prior written consent. Its [API documentation](https://verified.realestate/landlens/docs/price-map-transactions-api) describes loading deeds into one's own model, but does not by itself resolve the rights needed for this public app. Obtain appropriate written terms for model training, evaluation, storage, publication and bulk use before relying on it for those purposes. The service may return named parties, so never place raw responses, keys, personal identifiers or paid results in this public repository. Registered consideration may differ from total consideration.

Another provider, [India Property Register](https://indiapropertyregister.com/deeds), advertises deed indexes for several states, but its [terms](https://indiapropertyregister.com/terms) require a separate written licence for training, fine-tuning or evaluating AI models and for bulk redistribution. Its own [API guide](https://indiapropertyregister.com/blog/property-api-for-proptech-and-brokers) says consideration is often missing from deed indexes. It has not been contracted or independently checked for a usable Tamil Nadu historical price panel. Tamil Nadu's official [patta transfer history service](https://tn.nic.in/news/additional-land-records-services-launch-by-honourable-minister-for-revenue-and-disaster-management-tamil-nadu/) can help research transfers for specific survey numbers from 2016; a transfer record is not automatically a sale-price observation.

## Build the market history

1. Start with a defined land class, unit and geography in a few well-covered Tamil Nadu pilot districts. Work from original, lawfully obtained registered-sale records or a licensed de-identified feed, supplemented by permitted dated listings and documented auctions. Confirm whether each source can be stored and displayed. Request both older available extracts and incremental updates, including failures and coverage; do not assume the full state or every year is available.
2. Preserve for each observation its source identifier and URL, rights, publication/retrieval date, actual transaction/listing date if known, consideration or asking price, original area and unit, land class, administrative codes, approximate location and precision, and quality/missingness flags. Retain original values alongside normalized INR/sq-ft values. Restrict raw records containing personal identifiers and publish only appropriately aggregated or permitted data.
3. Match repeated ads and related records without turning them into extra independent observations. Validate units, parcel size and outliers, and review ambiguous village/place matches against official sources. Record both accepted and rejected matches; measure the error rate on an independently checked sample. Do not silently place an unmatched village at a guessed point.
4. In each location/time/land-class cell, show reported sale evidence, asking evidence and the official rate separately, with observation dates, ranges, sample counts and provenance where adequate. Insufficient data should remain insufficient. A listing is not a sale, an auction may be a reserve price, and the gap between a registered price and an asking price does not measure undisclosed payments.
5. If sufficiently many independently matched listings and subsequently verified closed transactions become available, evaluate a local asking-to-sale relationship by land class, period and area, with out-of-sample uncertainty and selection-bias checks. Until then, do not use a universal discount or markup as if measured.

## Assemble dated explanatory variables

Keep project announcement, approval, construction, opening and cancellation events, route/location, source and the date information became public. Obtain dated and geographically appropriate population/census, employment or industrial activity, land use, transportation/accessibility, policy, local supply and price-index data where genuinely available. A census count must retain its actual year; estimates for other years must be labelled estimates. Satellite observations and night lights can add context with sensor and quality caveats. Avoid using future observations to explain or predict the past, and do not infer project expenditure from registration alone.

An operational panel is keyed by geography, land class and observation period, with source/event/retrieval dates and dataset versions. Start with the variables that have measurable coverage and plausible timing; report missingness rather than fabricating annual values. Use compact incremental files or a database with restricted raw storage and a separate aggregated serving layer as the dataset grows; do not repeatedly commit the entire history or re-download all years. Source checks, checkpoints, bounded requests, deduplication and data-quality tests must precede state-by-state expansion.

## Evaluate before trusting forecasts or effects

Compare last observed values and simple local/district baselines with the current formula and any proposed ML model. Use rolling future holdouts and geographic blocks, keeping repeat listings and nearby correlated records together. Report sample sizes, median and tail errors, results by district and land class, and observed interval coverage and width. If the model does not beat simple baselines or evidence is too thin, do not present a validated numerical forecast.

For a road, factory or other investment, define the treated area, nearby comparison areas and announcement/opening dates in advance; check prior trends, other simultaneous projects, policy changes, geography, spillovers and property mix. Test across independent projects with uncertainty at the project/geography level. This can estimate an association under stated assumptions; where those assumptions or data fail, show scenarios and evidence instead of claiming a measured causal premium. Risk, opportunity and liquidity scores remain deferred until the price and historical evidence foundations are established and independently evaluated.
