# National Housing Bank housing-index sources

The [NHB RESIDEX dashboard](https://residex.nhbonline.org.in/) provides city housing indices, which describe housing markets and are not vacant-land prices or evidence of a project's causal effect. Different index bases, geographic coverage and methods should be considered separately.

On 13 November 2026, the app's previously copied NHB series were withdrawn and the scheduled NHB refresh was disabled. The reason is a reuse conflict that was missed in the initial review: NHB's general [website copyright policy](https://www.nhb.org.in/copyright-policy/) permits accurate reproduction with attribution, but the [RESIDEX portal disclaimer](https://residex.nhbonline.org.in/Dashboard/Disclaimer) and the NHB 2013–2017 RESIDEX booklet contain a publication-specific requirement for prior written permission to reproduce or store the publication. The scope of those terms in relation to all individual NHB sources has not been resolved. The current app links to NHB rather than republishing the extracted observations until the applicable terms are clarified.

The archive was published in earlier commits before this restriction was discovered. Removing files from the current branch and deployed site does not remove them from Git history or past copies. Any future use, including training, storage and public display, needs the appropriate source-specific rights and a new review. No copied NHB index observations were used to train or calibrate the land model.
